Vertiv Reports Strong Third Quarter 2026 with Net Sales Growth of 61%, Organic Orders Growth over 100% and Diluted EPS Growth of 91% (Adjusted Diluted EPS Growth of 78%); Raises Full Year 2026 Guidance Across All Key Metrics
Third Quarter 2026 Results
•Net sales of $4,313 million, 61% higher than third quarter 2025.
•Operating profit up 90% and adjusted operating profit(1) up 83% from third quarter 2025. Adjusted operating margin of 25.2%, up 290 basis points compared to third quarter 2025.
•Diluted EPS grew 91% to $1.95 and adjusted diluted EPS(1) grew 78% to $2.21 compared to third quarter 2025.
•Operating cash flow of $794 million and adjusted free cash flow(1) of $652 million, up 64% and 50%, respectively, compared to third quarter 2025.
Fourth Quarter 2026 Guidance
•Expects fourth quarter 2026 net sales of $4,413 million and organic sales growth of 50%, each at the midpoint of guidance, compared to fourth quarter 2025.
•Expects fourth quarter 2026 diluted EPS of $2.08 and adjusted diluted EPS of $2.25, each at the midpoint of guidance, an increase of 82% and 65%, respectively, compared to fourth quarter 2025.
Full Year 2026 Guidance
•Expects full year 2026 net sales of $14,650 million and organic sales growth of 38%, each at the midpoint of guidance, compared to full year 2025.
•Expects full year 2026 diluted EPS of $6.29 and adjusted diluted EPS of $7.15, each at the midpoint of guidance, an increase of 84% and 70%, respectively, compared to full year 2025.
COLUMBUS, Ohio Oct. 28, 2026 – Vertiv Holdings Co (NYSE: VRT) (“Vertiv”), a global leader in critical digital infrastructure, reported financial results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026. Vertiv reported third quarter net sales of $4,313 million, an increase of $1,637 million, or 61%, compared to third quarter 2025, reflecting 55% organic sales growth and approximately 6% contribution from acquisitions. Growth was broad-based across all regions, with the Americas up 75% (66% organic), APAC up 50% (48% organic), and EMEA up 23% (19% organic). Strong demand visibility, ongoing capacity investments, and disciplined execution give Vertiv the confidence to raise full year net sales guidance by $650 million, along with increases to other associated financial metrics.
Third quarter operating profit of $984 million increased $467 million and adjusted operating profit of $1,087 million increased $491 million, up 90% and 83%, respectively, from third quarter 2025. Adjusted operating margin was 25.2%, up 290 basis points compared to third quarter 2025, driven by operational leverage on higher sales and by manufacturing productivity, partially offset by mix impacts and continued growth investments.
"We are in the middle of the largest infrastructure buildout and it's accelerating," said Giordano Albertazzi, Vertiv's Chief Executive Officer. "The demands on data center infrastructure are evolving faster than ever, and Vertiv's role in that buildout keeps expanding. We're compressing new product development cycles from years to months. We're industrializing converged infrastructure at a scale that didn't exist before and strengthening competencies that are very hard to replicate. We are expanding capacity, improving productivity, and executing with rigor. The infrastructure we deliver performs at the highest levels of reliability. Customers are choosing Vertiv as the partner that can engineer, deliver, scale and service across the entire lifecycle. Our more than 100% organic orders growth year over year and our overall performance are clear testaments to that.”
"The companies that will define the digital age are the ones investing ahead of the curve and delivering for customers, and that is what we are doing," said Dave Cote, Vertiv's Executive Chairman. "We are in an industry where the more technology changes, the better it is for us. We keep investing, we keep building, and what we have built is designed to scale. I believe there is a long runway ahead and the opportunity only gets bigger from here.”



Adjusted Free Cash Flow and Liquidity
Net cash generated by operating activities in the third quarter was $794 million, and adjusted free cash flow was $652 million, increasing 64% and 50%, respectively, from third quarter 2025. Third quarter adjusted free cash flow was driven by higher adjusted operating profit partially offset by increased working capital and higher capital expenditures to support growth.
Vertiv’s liquidity increased to $6.2 billion in the third quarter, with the company maintaining a net cash position and further broadening its strategic flexibility. Capital expenditures for full year 2026 are still anticipated at approximately 4.0% of revenue, reflecting the continued investment Vertiv is making to capture increasing demand across its pipelines.
Updated Full Year and Fourth Quarter 2026 Guidance
The data center market continues to generate strong, broad-based global demand, further validating the depth and duration of the growth cycle. Vertiv continues to expand capacity and invest in next-generation technology to stay ahead of customer needs and capture an expanding opportunity set.

About Vertiv Holdings Co
Vertiv (NYSE: VRT) brings together hardware, software, analytics and ongoing services to enable its customers’ vital applications to run continuously, perform optimally and grow with their business needs. Vertiv solves the most important challenges facing today’s data centers, communication networks and commercial and industrial facilities with a portfolio of power, cooling and IT infrastructure solutions and services that extends from the cloud to the edge of the network. Headquartered in Westerville, Ohio, USA, Vertiv does business in more than 130 countries. For more information, and for the latest news and content from Vertiv, visit vertiv.com.

Reconciliation of GAAP and non-GAAP Financial Measures

To supplement this news release, we have included certain non-GAAP financial measures in the format of performance metrics. Management believes these non-GAAP financial measures provide investors with additional meaningful financial information that should be considered when assessing our underlying business performance and trends. Further, management believes these non-GAAP financial measures also enhance investors' ability to compare period-to-period financial results. Non-GAAP financial measures should be viewed in addition to, and not as an alternative for, the company's reported results prepared in accordance with GAAP. Our non-GAAP financial measures do not represent a comprehensive basis of accounting. Therefore, our non-GAAP financial measures may not be comparable to similarly titled measures reported by other companies. Reconciliations of each of these non-GAAP financial measures to GAAP information are also included. Management uses these non-GAAP financial measures in making financial, operating, compensation and planning decisions and in evaluating the company's performance. Disclosing these non-GAAP financial measures allows investors and management to view our operating results excluding the impact of items that are not reflective of the underlying operating performance.

Vertiv’s non-GAAP financial measures include:

•Adjusted operating profit (loss), which represents operating profit (loss), adjusted to exclude amortization of intangibles, restructuring costs associated with the global restructuring program, contingent consideration and merger and acquisition costs;
•Adjusted operating margin, which represents adjusted operating profit (loss) divided by net sales;
•Organic net sales growth, which represents the change in net sales adjusted to exclude the impacts of foreign currency exchange rate and acquisitions [without comparable sales detail in both periods presented];
•Adjusted free cash flow, which represents net cash provided by (used for) operating activities adjusted to exclude capital expenditures and investments in capitalized software; and
•Adjusted diluted EPS, which represents diluted earnings per share adjusted to exclude amortization of intangibles, restructuring costs associated with the global restructuring program, contingent consideration and merger and acquisition costs, and the costs related to the March 3, 2026 repayment of the Term Loan Credit Agreement and the associated interest rate swaps being settled.
