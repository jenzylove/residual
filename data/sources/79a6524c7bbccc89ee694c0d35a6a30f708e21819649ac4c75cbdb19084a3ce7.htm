PRESS RELEASE

COHERENT CORP. REPORTS FIRST QUARTER FISCAL 2027 RESULTS

 

  •  

Q1 REVENUE OF $2.37B, INCREASED 50% Y/Y AND 56% Y/Y ON A PRO FORMA BASIS

 

  •  

Q1 GAAP GROSS MARGIN OF 39.1%, INCREASED 254 bps Y/Y; Q1 NON-GAAP GROSS MARGIN OF 40.6%, INCREASED 184 bps Y/Y

 

  •  

Q1 GAAP EPS OF $1.90, INCREASED $0.71 Y/Y; Q1 NON-GAAP EPS OF $2.09, INCREASED $0.93 Y/Y

SAXONBURG, Pa., November 4, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) – Coherent Corp. (NYSE: COHR) (“Coherent,” “We,” or the “Company”), a global leader in photonics, announced financial results today for its first quarter of fiscal year 2027 ended September 30, 2026.

Revenue for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 was $2.37 billion, with GAAP gross margin of 39.1% and GAAP net income of $1.90 per diluted share. On a non-GAAP basis, gross margin was 40.6% with net income per diluted share of $2.09.

“Q1 marked a strong start to fiscal 2027, with pro forma revenue up 56% year over year and non-GAAP EPS up 80%,” said Jim Anderson, CEO. “Our established businesses delivered strong growth, while new growth areas are beginning to ramp. As AI datacenters transition from copper to optical connectivity, Coherent’s broad photonics platform and manufacturing scale position us to help customers meet rising needs for bandwidth and energy efficiency. We expect fiscal 2027 pro forma revenue growth to exceed 60%.”

Sherri Luther, CFO, said, “Our Q1 results delivered substantial operating leverage and durable gross margin expansion, driven by pricing optimization and cost improvements. This margin strength gives us the confidence to raise our longer-term target. We expect to achieve our first quarter with non-GAAP gross margin above 45% before the end of calendar 2028.”

About Coherent

Coherent is the global photonics leader. We harness photons to drive innovation. Industry leaders in the datacenter, communications, and industrial markets rely on Coherent’s world-leading technology to fuel their own innovation and growth.

Founded in 1971 and operating in more than 20 countries, Coherent brings the industry’s broadest, deepest technology stack; unmatched supply chain resilience; and global scale to help its customers solve their toughest technology challenges. For more information, please visit us at coherent.com.
