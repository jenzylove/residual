How it works

Four steps, every release

1

Read the filing

Revenue and guidance come from the SEC press release. Each number keeps its exact quote and a document hash.

2

Remove the market

Hourly Bitget betas split the early move into market, sector and liquidity. What is left is the company.

3

Check the gates

Hedge fit, liquidity, cost, robustness and a quote checked AI read. One failure means no trade.

4

Trade the pair

Long or short the company against a beta weighted hedge, with fees, slippage and funding on both legs.
